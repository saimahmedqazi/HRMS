# Plan 02-01: Departments, Designations & Teams Management

**Phase:** 02 — Organization Hierarchy, Employee Management & Cloud Storage  
**Status:** Completed  

## Objective
Implement backend CRUD APIs and service logic for Departments, Designations, and Teams with department manager and team lead assignments, tenant isolation, and audit logging.

## Tasks
1. **Departments Module**:
   - `GET /api/v1/departments` — List departments in tenant with employee counts and manager details.
   - `POST /api/v1/departments` — Create department (`COMPANY_ADMIN`, `HR_MANAGER`).
   - `GET /api/v1/departments/:id` — Get department details with teams and member roster.
   - `PATCH /api/v1/departments/:id` — Update department details and assign `managerId`.
   - `DELETE /api/v1/departments/:id` — Soft-delete department (verify no active employees assigned).
2. **Designations Module**:
   - `GET /api/v1/designations` — List designations with employee count.
   - `POST /api/v1/designations` — Create designation.
   - `PATCH /api/v1/designations/:id` — Update designation.
   - `DELETE /api/v1/designations/:id` — Delete designation.
3. **Teams Module**:
   - `GET /api/v1/teams` — List teams within tenant/department.
   - `POST /api/v1/teams` — Create team under department with `leadId`.
   - `GET /api/v1/teams/:id` — Get team with lead and member roster.
   - `PATCH /api/v1/teams/:id` — Update team and lead assignment.
   - `DELETE /api/v1/teams/:id` — Delete team.
4. **Unit & Integration Tests**:
   - Test department CRUD, manager assignment, and constraint guards.
   - Test designation uniqueness per tenant.
   - Test team hierarchy under department.
