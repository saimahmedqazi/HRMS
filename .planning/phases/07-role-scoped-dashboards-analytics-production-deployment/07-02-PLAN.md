# Plan 07-02: 7 Customized Role-Scoped Dashboards & Recharts Visualizations

**Phase:** 07 — Role-Scoped Dashboards, Analytics & Production Deployment  
**Status:** In Progress  

## Objective
Implement 7 customized role-scoped dashboard views in `/dashboard` utilizing interactive Recharts graphs for attendance trends, department distribution, and compensation expenditure.

## Tasks
1. **Interactive Charts (`Recharts`)**:
   - `AttendanceTrendsChart`: Responsive AreaChart showing Onsite vs Remote vs Absent.
   - `DepartmentHeadcountChart`: BarChart showing employee allocation across departments.
2. **Dynamic Role-Based Dashboard (`/dashboard/page.tsx`)**:
   - Dispatches view based on `user.role`:
     - `SUPER_ADMIN`: Platform tenants, MRR metrics, company status breakdown.
     - `OPERATION_MANAGER`: Multi-tenant operations, pending hardware requisitions.
     - `COMPANY_ADMIN`: Executive KPI cards, monthly payroll expenditure, attendance trend chart, quick actions.
     - `HR_MANAGER`: Workforce overview, probation alerts, attendance exceptions queue banner, pending leave approvals.
     - `DEPARTMENT_MANAGER`: Department attendance, team leave approvals, department payroll shortcut.
     - `TEAM_LEAD`: Squad live presence, active work timers, break overstay notices.
     - `EMPLOYEE`: Punch in/out widget, live digital work timer, leave balances cards, latest payslip download.
3. **Typechecks & UI Verification**:
