# Plan 07-01: Analytics Backend Engine & Role-Scoped Overview Endpoints

**Phase:** 07 — Role-Scoped Dashboards, Analytics & Production Deployment  
**Status:** In Progress  

## Objective
Implement the backend `AnalyticsModule` providing role-scoped operational metrics, attendance trend aggregation, department headcount breakdown, and platform-level Super Admin telemetry.

## Tasks
1. **Analytics Service (`AnalyticsService`)**:
   - `getDashboardOverview(companyId, user)`:
     - Scoped metrics:
       - `SUPER_ADMIN`: Total companies, total employees, subscription tiers breakdown, support mode metrics.
       - `COMPANY_ADMIN`: Headcount, monthly payroll total, today's attendance summary (Present, Remote, Absent), 7-day attendance trend, department headcount breakdown.
       - `HR_MANAGER`: Headcount, active probation count, pending leave requests, attendance exceptions pending review.
       - `DEPARTMENT_MANAGER`: Department headcount, today's attendance, pending approvals.
       - `TEAM_LEAD`: Team size, team members present today, active work timers, break alerts.
       - `EMPLOYEE`: Today's punch status, active timer minutes, leave balances summary, latest payslip info.
2. **Analytics Controller**:
   - `GET /api/v1/analytics/overview`: Unified scoped endpoint for all dashboard roles.
   - `GET /api/v1/analytics/super-admin`: Super Admin platform telemetry.
3. **Unit Tests**:
   - `analytics.service.spec.ts`: Tests metrics calculation and role scoping.
