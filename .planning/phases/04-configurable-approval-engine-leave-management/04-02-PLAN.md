# Plan 04-02: Leave Request Workflows, Prescription Uploads & HR Modal

**Phase:** 04 — Configurable Approval Engine & Leave Management  
**Status:** In Progress  

## Objective
Implement end-to-end leave workflows: submission with medical prescription upload, balance deduction, multi-step approval routing, and prescription retrieval endpoint for HR.

## Tasks
1. **Leave Management Service**:
   - `POST /api/v1/leave/requests`:
     - Validates probation gating.
     - Validates leave balance sufficiency.
     - Enforces medical prescription upload for SICK leave (via Multer + StorageService).
     - Resolves approval chain.
     - Updates `pendingDays` in `LeaveBalance`.
     - Dispatches email and in-app notifications.
   - `GET /api/v1/leave/requests`: Scoped list of leave requests.
   - `GET /api/v1/leave/balances`: Current employee leave balances (allocated, used, pending).
   - `POST /api/v1/leave/requests/:id/step1`: Step 1 approver decision (`APPROVE` or `REJECT`).
   - `POST /api/v1/leave/requests/:id/step2`: Final approver decision (`APPROVE` or `REJECT`).
     - On final approval: transfers `pendingDays` to `usedDays`.
   - `POST /api/v1/leave/requests/:id/cancel`: Requester can cancel pending leave.
   - `GET /api/v1/leave/requests/:id/prescription`: Direct prescription view/download endpoint for HR.
2. **Unit & Integration Tests**:
   - Leave submission with sick prescription test.
   - Leave balance deduction test on approval.
   - Rejection and cancellation restoration test.
